# Battle Tanks LAN — Combat Field Manual & Gameplay Guide

Welcome, Commander. This manual details vehicle operations, demolition munitions, terrain exploitation, and tactical victory conditions.

## 2. Weapons Systems

### The Demolition Bomb (`ENTER`)
- **Role**: Heavy siege weapon & area denial.
- **Operation**: Pressing `ENTER` lobs an explosive bomb in the tank's facing direction.
- **Detonation**: Explodes upon contact with solid obstacles or when its 28-tick fuse expires.
- **Blast Radius**: 54px circular AOE shockwave.
- **Effect**:
  - Vaporizes all brick sub-tiles in range.
  - Obliterates indestructible steel blocks.
  - Destroys enemy shells caught within the blast.
  - Inflicts 2-life damage on tanks caught in the explosion.
  - 1-hit kill on Eagle HQs.
- **Cooldown**: 2.5 seconds (tracked by the glowing HUD gauge).

### Cannon Shell (`SPACE`)
- High-velocity armor-piercing projectile.
- Destroys 16×16 sub-quadrants of brick blocks.
- Cancels enemy shells in mid-air upon head-to-head contact.

## 3. Terrain Tactics

1. **Brick Walls (Terracotta)**:
   - Carved into 4 distinct quadrants. Use shells to punch narrow sniper slits to eliminate tanks without exposing your vehicle.
2. **Steel Blocks (White / Metallic Plate)**:
   - Deflects regular shells. Can only be destroyed by **Bombs** or **Tier 4 Super Tanks**.
3. **Water Moats (Deep Blue)**:
   - Impassable by vehicles. However, shells and bombs travel across water freely! Use water channels for long-range sniper ambushes.
4. **Forest / Foliage (Lush Camo Green)**:
   - Conceals your tank. Tanks inside appear with ghosted transparency to the owner, but are virtually invisible to enemies outside the foliage until firing.
5. **Ice Tracks (Crystalline Cyan)**:
   - Slippery surface with reduced friction. Releasing movement keys causes the tank to drift along its momentum vector.
6. **The Eagle HQ (Golden Phoenix)**:
   - Your team's command center. If struck by any shell or bomb, the Eagle is destroyed into ruins and your team suffers **immediate defeat**.

## 4. Powerups

- ⭐ **Star**: Upgrades tank tier (Tier 1: Scout, Tier 2: Double Armor, Tier 3: Rapid Fire, Tier 4: Steel Piercer).
- 💣 **Bomb Crate**: Instantly resets bomb cooldown for rapid demolition.
- 🛡️ **Shield**: 10-second invulnerability forcefield bubble.
- ⛏️ **Shovel**: "Iron Wall" — reinforces the brick fortress around your Eagle HQ into solid steel for 15 seconds.
- ⏱️ **Clock**: Temporarily freezes opposing tanks in place for 5 seconds.
- ❤️ **Life**: Adds +1 reserve tank life.

---

## 5. Game Modes

### Mode 1: Attack + Defence (Eagle Protection)
- Each team has a fortified Eagle HQ.
- Protect your Eagle while breaching enemy fortifications to obliterate theirs.
- Destroying an enemy Eagle instantly eliminates that squad from the match.

### Mode 2: Team Elimination (Deathmatch)
- No Eagle HQs.
- Complete annihilation of all opposing vehicles.
- Teams fight until only one surviving squad remains.
